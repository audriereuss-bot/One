# Firm Foundations — calm editorial wellness

Source: the owner's own 6-page guide reference (Firm-Foundations-6-pages.pdf,
built in `/home/user/One`). This is the owner's house ebook style.

## Mood
Warm cream paper, deep navy blocks, light italic serif headings in taupe,
sunlit natural photography cut on sharp diagonals. Calm, premium, approachable.

## Essentials
- Cream canvas `#F3EEE7`, navy `#14284B`, taupe heading `#7A6352`, dividers `#CFC6BA`, body `#2E2E2E`.
- Headings: **Cormorant Garamond Italic 400**, line-height ~1.0, tracking -0.01em.
- Eyebrow label top-left in **Montserrat 500**, ALL CAPS, tracking ~0.34em, followed by a 1.5px navy rule running toward the right margin.
- Body: **DM Sans 400**, line-height 1.6.
- Item titles (pillars/steps): Montserrat 600 caps, tracking 0.26em, navy.
- Signature device: **photos with angled cuts** — rectangle photo with cream triangles overlaid to cut diagonal edges, plus **navy triangles/bands** anchoring a corner or bottom edge.

## Page recipes
1. **Cover/Intro** — heading upper left, body below, photo in the right ~40% with a diagonal left edge, navy triangle bottom-left carrying a tracked white two-line tagline.
2. **Overview list** — heading + intro, then 6 rows: navy icon circle · caps title · one-line description · hairline divider.
3. **Big statement** — large 2-line heading + short paragraph top half; photo fills the bottom half with a diagonal top edge and a small cream notch.
4. **How-to steps** — photo strip down the right edge (angled); numbered outline circles with title/description and hairlines.
5. **Quote page** — full navy page, cream serif quote mark, centered italic quote, short cream rule, lighter-navy diagonal slab at the bottom.
6. **Framework/section opener** — heading, wide photo with jagged diagonal top, caption, small arrow bottom-right, thin navy band at page foot.

## Writing
Calm, encouraging, practical. "Simple habits. Lasting results." Short promise, one supportive sentence. No hype.

## Do / Don't
- Do keep photos warm and natural (desert, palms, citrus, track & sky).
- Do keep navy to blocks and icons; never navy body text.
- Don't use bold serif; don't round corners; don't add drop shadows.
