# Slash — serif in the shadows

Inspiration only. Never reuse the name, artwork, logos, quotes, prices or figures.

## Mood
Predominantly black. White high-contrast editorial serif with one meaningful
word in true italic. Silver objects, smoked glass, charcoal "display cases",
gold reserved for numbers and a warm grainy horizon at the close.

## Essentials
- Canvas `#040406`; panels gradient `#121317 → #0b0c0e` (201°); edges `#2e3038`; rule `#1c1d22`.
- Text greys: body `#acafb9`, muted `#9194a1`, subtle `#777a88`, faint `#5e616e`. White only for promises/labels/controls.
- Gold `#ae9357` / champagne `#fff0cc` / amber `#cd8939` — numerals only. Copper `#cc9166` for focus rings + editorial categories. Warm horizon `#2e2a26`.
- **IvyPresto 400** (fallback **Bodoni Moda**), tracking **+0.01em**: chapter 88/1, display 64/1.05, heading 64/1, small heading 52/1.125, quote 44/1.375.
- **Inter** for UI/body at -0.02em: lead 20/1.5, body 18/1.375, card copy 18 w300, small 16, button 15 w600, nav 14.
- Container 1216px (32px padding); chapter entry 224px; grids 12px or 24px gaps.

## Components
- Opening email shell 400×48, radius 10, border `#2e3038`, white 40px button radius 6 inset.
- Centered email pill 520×60 with white 48px pill action inside.
- Nav action: 32px outlined pill `#464853`.
- Capability case 368×418, radius 10, art top, caption bottom (white 500 label + grey 300 explanation). Masked rim overlay: 1px white 60%, overlay blend, fading downward.
- Question rows: 24px Inter 500, -0.04em, bottom rule `#1c1d22`, chevron.

## Signature devices
- **Cinematic object stage** — full-bleed black, oblique lit object lower-right, diagonal white light wedges, vignette, bottom fade. CSS fallback: perspective-tilted dark plane with 1px translucent rim on a charcoal slab.
- **Metallic numerals** — 103° gradient #ae9357 → #fff0cc 40% → #ae9357 70%, clipped to text; blurred amber glow below.
- **Warm grain horizon** — ~600px band fading black → #2e2a26, noise tile at 0.3 opacity masked toward bottom; 64px serif CTA + email pill.

## Page flow
Cinematic opening (left copy + email shell + logos) → customer photo gallery → italic-accented chapters + display-case mosaics → quote + gold metrics → plans/security → split FAQ → warm grain conversion → five-column carbon footer with disclosures.

## Writing
"You / your"; headline carries flourish, next sentence carries specifics. Italicise the word that changes meaning.

## Don't
Gold buttons · bold sans headlines · solid white outlines on cards · everything white text · ending right after the CTA.

## Ebook translation
Black pages, serif chapter titles with one italic word, charcoal "case" boxes for key takeaways, gold only on statistics, final page uses the warm grain horizon. Ideal for premium/high-ticket business ebooks.
