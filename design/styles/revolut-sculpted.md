# Revolut — everyday life, sculpted

Inspiration only. Never reuse the name, artwork, rates, counts or claims.

## Mood
Aspirational consumer product. Sunlit lifestyle photography against blue sky,
then dramatic black stages where everyday things become metallic/glass
sculptures. Rounded geometric grotesk, medium weight, close-set.

## Essentials
- Ink `#1f1f1f`, white, stage black `#000`, utility footer `#191c1f`, muted `#717173`, soft tile `#f7f7f7`, footer text `#c9c9cd`. Sky gradient `#4c8acc → #3991d8`.
- **Aeonik Pro 500** (fallback **DM Sans**): display 88/1 -0.02em (6.11vw), heading 51.8/1 -0.0116em (clamp 3rem…3.75rem), card heading 24/28 -0.01em, support 18/24 w400, action 16/22.
- **Inter** body 16/1.5, caption 14/22, utility 12.
- Buttons: 42px capsule, padding 10px 24px, radius 9999 — dark on light, white on black, 2px white outline for secondary.
- Content 1000px centred; stages full-bleed 810–900px tall; tiles radius 20, padding 24; 8px spacing rhythm.

## Signature devices
- **Sky portrait + floating result** — full-bleed portrait on sky, white headline upper-left, one transparent rounded result card (2px rgba(255,255,255,.6) border) over the torso.
- **Sculptural product object** — oversized brushed-metal/glass object on black filling lower two-thirds. SVG fallback: two perspective rounded rects, black→silver gradient, translucent rim, darker offset for thickness, cool radial glow.
- **Soft cool light** — elliptical radial rgba(150,190,198,.5) → transparent under the object, blurred 64px.
- **Monumental metric** (single-number chapter) — 240px numeral on pale blue wash.

## Page flow
Immersive human opening → award/rating strip → alternating lifestyle splits and black object stages (one promise each) → centred closing line + dark capsule → charcoal utility band with plan tiles (3 + 2), six link columns, social row, legal.

## Writing
Short, benefit-first, light wordplay: "Savings, on demand." Supporting line lists concrete verbs. Buttons = next action ("Get started").

## Don't
Bold 700 headlines · blue buttons because the sky is blue · shadows on all tiles · tiny logo-only footer · display face for long text.

## Ebook translation
Cover = lifestyle photo with one floating "result" card; chapter openers = black page with one sculpted object; tips in #f7f7f7 20px-radius tiles. Great for app companions, consumer guides, membership welcome books.
