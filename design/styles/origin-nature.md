# Origin — nature behind intelligence

Inspiration only. Never reuse the name, logo, testimonials, awards or claims.

## Mood
Reassuring and personal. Luminous nature photography (sky, meadow, flowers,
dunes) and delicate light serif headlines frame dark, finely detailed product
panels. Near-black middle, colour concentrated in scenes.

## Essentials
- Canvas `#050505`; cards `rgba(255,255,255,.04)` fill + `rgba(255,255,255,.08)` 1px edge; divider 14% white.
- Text: white, heading `#fafafa`, support `#9b9b9b` (60%), muted `#555` (32%).
- Atmosphere accents: blue `#219ff8`, violet `#847dff`, ochre `#cc7c01`, meadow `#61a133`, earth `#a7612e` — only as blurred glows/seams.
- **Lyon Display 300** (fallback **Cormorant Garamond 300**) with true italic for one phrase: hero 96/1.15, chapter 80/1 (-0.01em), closing 96/0.9, promise 48/1.15.
- **Inter 300** body 16/1.5; card title 20/1.08. **Roboto Mono** 12px caps for nav + buttons only.
- Buttons: 48px tall, **8px radius**, white fill, black mono label + small arrow. Secondary: 10% white fill.
- Containers: stage 1400px/32px padding; heading wrap 980; opening wrap 830; support 420. Panels 24px radius.

## Signature devices
- **Atmospheric nature panel** — cover photo, dark overlay, 541px circular glow near lower centre (blur 60) tinted ochre/green/earth, 24px radius, copy on top.
- **Luminous seam** — 1435×696 blue→violet gradient blurred 50px behind a clipped dark wrapper so only an edge glows.
- **Device/interface theatre** — tall phone or a dark framed outcome card with a few floating data fragments; lots of negative space.
- **Advice glass card** — mono category, light serif outcome, short sans explanation, faint violet radial in corner.

## Page flow
Sky opening (centered promise, white button, oversized phone rising from below) → promise + validation band → interface carousel (420px cards, 48px padding) → notification stage → alternating nature/interface pairs (676×631, 24px gap) → review wall (plain, stars) → image-backed 96px closing with italic action word → utility footer (newsletter 400px + link columns).

## Writing
Warm, outcome-led: "Track your entire financial life." Verb + concrete outcome; italic on the promise word.

## Don't
Mono for paragraphs · heavy sans headlines · coloured pill buttons · violet canvas · shadows everywhere · tiny nature thumbnails.

## Ebook translation
Chapter openers = full-bleed nature photo with light serif title; content pages dark with 4% panels for exercises; mono only for small labels ("CHAPTER 02"). Perfect for coaching, mindfulness, personal-finance or wellness-tech guides.
