# Vivid — prismatic manifesto

Inspiration only. Never reuse the brand name, people, clients, artwork or claims.

## Mood
Cinematic near-black. Oversized *regular-weight* grotesk statements in warm
white. Colour exists only as refraction: glass cube sculptures with rainbow
edges and a soft blue-violet halo around a dark centre. Conceptual, confident.

## Essentials
- Canvas `#101010`, text `#fffdf9` (warm white). Rules: warm `rgba(255,253,249,.2)`, cool `rgba(111,135,156,.6)`.
- **Neue Montreal 400** everywhere (fallback **DM Sans**). Hierarchy by size only.
- Opening display 135.9px/1, -0.02em, starts ~¼ across the viewport; lead 33.5px/1.2 starts at mid-page below it.
- Statements 105.3px/1.01; heading 55.9px/1.125; section label 32px caps; body 20px/1.2; caption 15px.
- 8-column grid, 38.4px gutters, main content 1070px (≈185px insets at 1440). Support copy shifted right.
- Actions are typographic: underlined inline link; nav contact is a thin outlined rectangle. No filled pill CTAs.

## Signature devices
- **Refractive modular sculpture** — 4–6 SVG cubes (3 faces each, ~110px), stepped cluster ~300×400, rotated ~20°. Faces near-canvas at low opacity, top edges warm-white 1–3px, blurred blue edge duplicates (6–12px), thin red→yellow→cyan→blue strips on 1–2 bevels. Sits behind live text.
- **Peripheral spectral halo** — 2–3 huge (≈1200×1000) blurred elliptical strokes (30–80px, blur 40–100px) in blue/cyan/violet, dark radial mask in the middle, faint noise. Strength varies per chapter.

## Section recipes
Offset opening → offset manifesto pairs (statement + right-shifted lead, ~79px gap) → three open capability modules (331px cols, bottom rule, 11px symbol + caps label) → evidence ledger (tall rows, caps labels, cool rules) + small mono logos (140×80 cells) → portrait strip (343×406, 15px radius, staggered, edge-clipped) + indented accordion (886px, ~161px rows) → plus-joined monumental close beside a 331px lead ending in an underlined link → email + social mark + tiny legal row.

## Motion
Line-by-line mask reveals, 0.5s cubic-bezier(0.52,0.01,0,1), staggered.

## Writing
"We / our"; declarative, parallel phrasing; plus signs join short concepts. Actions lowercase ("start a conversation").

## Don't
Bold display type · rainbow on text or buttons · rounded cards everywhere · drop shadows · sharp neon ring halos · collapsing the 33.5px lead into 16px body.

## Ebook translation
Dark full-bleed pages; chapter openers are one monumental regular statement with a glass object; content pages use warm-white body on #101010 with cool hairline lists. Great for premium manifestos/brand books.
