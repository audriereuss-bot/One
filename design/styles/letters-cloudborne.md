# Letters — cloudborne everyday tools

Inspiration only. Never reuse the name, logo, medical examples, testimonials, figures or pricing.

## Mood
Airy, friendly productivity. Powder-blue sky stages, rounded sans with tight
tracking, white paper sculptures and small floating interface fragments on
soft grey panels. Admin made weightless.

## Essentials
- Sky gradient `#779bc1 0% → #9abfda 58% → #cbdfec 100%`; accent `#2597d0` (small details only).
- Ink `#070709`, headings `#151515`, body `#60606c`, muted `#868c98`, panel `#f5f5f5`, soft panel `#fafafa`, edge `#f4f4f4`.
- **Open Runde** (fallback **Nunito Sans**): display 80/0.9 w600 -0.04em · heading 44/1.1 w600 -0.04em · card heading 28/1.2 w500 -0.03em · support heading 20/1.4 · body 18/1.4 · action 17 w600 · detail 16 · footer 14. Inter for metadata.
- Primary button: 48px, radius 100, `#070709` fill, layered soft shadow. Secondary: white with chevron.
- Radii: stages & feature panels 48, testimonials 40, plans 32, floating UI 18. Sky stage sits inside a 12px white frame.
- Feature panels 650×646, padding 40, gap 36; benefits 3-up (~421px).

## Signature devices
- **White workflow sculpture** — SVG open envelope/container with white document cards fanning out, soft rgba(119,155,193,.15) shadows, sparse white specks on the sky; clipped at the stage bottom.
- **Floating interface vignettes** — 2–3 white rounded cards (10–18px), muted lines, one #2597d0 detail, bottom fade.
- **Dimensional word tiles** — 48px white rounded square (7px radius) with a pale blue glyph, embedded inside a heading.
- **Cloud plan island** — sky island with clouds framing three plans; the middle plan rises higher.

## Page flow
Sky opening (centred 2-line promise, support, black button, sculpture) → paired capability panels + 3 benefit cards → isolated product interlude → tabbed demo frame → numbered workflow rail → testimonials (120px circle portraits, handwritten sign-off) → assurance cards + trust object → sky plan island → product-ensemble close → white footer (logo left, 4 columns right).

## Writing
"Patients, not paperwork" pattern: what you value vs friction removed. One semibold benefit phrase inline. Actions: "Sign up for free".

## Don't
Navy/dark theme · blue buttons · 8px corners · heavy shadows on grey panels · uppercase eyebrows everywhere · dark footer.

## Ebook translation
Cover = sky stage with a white paper object; content on white with #f5f5f5 48px-radius callout panels; checklists as floating white cards. Best for templates, planners, Notion/Canva product guides, "done-for-you" toolkits.
